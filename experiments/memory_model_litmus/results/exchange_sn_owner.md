## sn_owner

The owner exchange of the sn table (256 KiB partials, `wt` synchronisation, each group on one XCD, 30 launches after a 1 GiB read each) in two orders. Interleaved, as in sn: the owner waits for a peer's flag, reads that peer's partial, then moves to the next; its sync phase ends at the first flag and its read phase holds the later flag waits. Flags first, as v20's GEMM: the owner waits for every peer's flag, then reads every partial; its sync phase is the whole flag wait and its read phase the reads alone. Read per peer: read / (n - 1).
