# v9-v20 on the stream-K test shapes

bf16, no bias, v9 tile order, GPU 0. TFLOPS from the median rocprof kernel time over 1000 launches on a rotating 512 MB of inputs (cold caches); where a case was timed in several processes (`repeat`), the median of those. L2 hit rate is TCC_HIT / (HIT + MISS) over warm launches 3-10 on the same inputs. Generated by `sweep.py report`.

## 1. Shapes and winners

d = k-tiles * S / 256. "Runner-up" is the fastest configuration from another version (v20's variants count as one version); it often runs the same schedule as the best. "Next schedule" is the fastest configuration that runs a different schedule, which is the margin the schedule itself wins by. With S = 0 every configuration is data-parallel. "Spread" is the difference between the slowest and fastest of a configuration's processes, median / max over the configurations; margins below it are not significant.
